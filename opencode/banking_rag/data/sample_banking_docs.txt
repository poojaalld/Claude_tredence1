=== DOCUMENT 1: Account Types ===

Title: Types of Bank Accounts
Date: 2024-01-15
Author: Customer Service Department

We offer several types of accounts to meet your financial needs:

1. Savings Account
- Best for storing money and earning interest
- Minimum balance: $100
- Interest rate: 2.5% APY (Annual Percentage Yield)
- No monthly maintenance fee
- Limited withdrawals per month (6 free withdrawals)
- FDIC insured up to $250,000

2. Checking Account
- Designed for frequent daily transactions
- Minimum balance: $50
- No interest earned
- Monthly maintenance fee: $12.99 (waived with direct deposit)
- Unlimited transactions
- Includes free debit card and checks
- FDIC insured up to $250,000

3. Money Market Account
- Hybrid account combining features of savings and checking
- Minimum balance: $2,500
- Interest rate: 3.75% APY
- 6 free withdrawals per statement period
- Tiered interest rates based on balance
- FDIC insured up to $250,000

4. Certificate of Deposit (CD)
- Fixed-term investment products
- Minimum deposit: $1,000
- Terms: 3 months to 5 years
- Early withdrawal penalty: 6 months interest
- Interest rates: 4.5% - 5.0% depending on term
- FDIC insured up to $250,000

=== DOCUMENT 2: Loan Products ===

Title: Available Loan Products and Terms
Date: 2024-01-18
Author: Lending Department

We offer the following loan products:

Personal Loans
- Loan amounts: $1,000 - $50,000
- Interest rates: 6.5% - 18.5% APR
- Repayment terms: 12 - 60 months
- Application process: Online or in-branch
- Approval time: 24-48 hours
- No prepayment penalty

Auto Loans
- Vehicle types: Cars, trucks, motorcycles
- Loan amounts: $5,000 - $100,000
- Interest rates: 3.99% - 8.99% APR
- Repayment terms: 24 - 72 months
- Loan-to-value (LTV): Up to 125%
- Acceptable vehicles: Model year 2010 or newer
- No early payoff penalty

Home Loans (Mortgages)
- Loan amounts: $50,000 - $1,000,000+
- Interest rates: 6.5% - 7.5% APR (fixed)
- Repayment terms: 15 - 30 years
- Down payment: Minimum 3-20%
- Closing costs: 2-5% of loan amount
- PMI required if down payment < 20%
- Application process: Professional mortgage advisors available

Business Loans
- Loan amounts: $10,000 - $500,000
- Interest rates: 5.5% - 12% APR
- Terms: 2 - 10 years
- Purpose: Working capital, equipment, expansion
- Requires business plan and financial statements
- SBA loans available

=== DOCUMENT 3: Fees and Charges ===

Title: Schedule of Banking Fees and Charges
Date: 2024-01-20
Author: Finance Department

Standard Account Fees:
- Monthly account maintenance: $0 - $12.99
- Overdraft fee: $35 per occurrence
- NSF (Non-Sufficient Funds) fee: $35
- Wire transfer fee: $15 - $25 (domestic), $45 - $65 (international)
- ATM fee (out-of-network): $2.50 - $3.50
- Stop payment fee: $25
- Check copy fee: $2 per check
- Statement copy fee: $5

Card Fees:
- Credit card annual fee: $0 - $95 (depending on tier)
- Late payment fee: $25 - $40
- Over-limit fee: $35
- Foreign transaction fee: 2-3% of transaction
- Card replacement fee: $0 - $15

Loan Fees:
- Application fee: $0 - $500
- Origination fee: 0% - 3% of loan amount
- Appraisal fee: $250 - $500
- Title insurance: 0.5 - 1% of loan amount
- Early payoff penalty: None for most personal loans
